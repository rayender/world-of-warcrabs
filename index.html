How to play

Field manual

Summon

Pick a crab card, then tap a tile. Each crab costs shrimp and has a recharge time.

Shrimp

Shrimp swim across the field. Tap them before they leave. Krill Dredgers also find shrimp for you.

Scrap & upgrades

Beaten enemies drop scrap. Tap a deployed crab to spend scrap on upgrades. Its shell changes color with each level.

Waves

Every battle has 3 waves. Survive them and your army marches right to the next battle, upgrades and all.

Fortress

Battle 4 is the enemy fortress. It keeps sending troops until you destroy it. That clears the chapter.

Bosses

After the fortress's last wave, its commander comes out and shields the fortress. Red and green markers show where it will strike. It enrages below 40% health.

War Room

You can bring 8 crab types. Winning battles unlocks new crabs to swap in.
